2. Purpose of the Document
This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the scope, structure, content, design direction, and functionality required to design and develop a professional personal portfolio website for Guruvarya Shri Prakashbhau Shinde (Mathadhipati) — spiritual guru, astrologer, and spiritual counsellor — including a dedicated section/page for his charitable organisation, Sarvatmak Maharudra Parivar Trust. This document is intended to guide designers, developers, and content owners through a single, agreed source of truth before design and development begin.
3. Project Background
Guruvarya Shri Prakashbhau Shinde has dedicated over 14 years to the intense worship (tapasya) of Lord Shri Hanuman and is regarded as blessed with the grace of the Dasha Mahavidya (10 Mahavidyas). He holds a Doctorate in Spiritual Education, serves as Mathadhipati, and is recognised as an astrologer and spiritual counsellor. He is also the founding/guiding force behind Sarvatmak Maharudra Parivar Trust (Reg. No. E0009721(PUN)), a registered charitable trust engaged in social, educational, and humanitarian work.
The client requires a single professional website that:
●	Establishes his personal spiritual identity, credentials, and services online.
●	Builds trust and credibility with devotees and prospective clients seeking astrology/spiritual consultation.
●	Showcases his social work and the activities of Sarvatmak Maharudra Parivar Trust.
●	Provides an easy way for devotees/clients to get in touch, book consultations, and connect on WhatsApp.
4. Goals & Objectives
●	Create a premium, spiritually-rooted, trustworthy visual identity for the website.
●	Clearly present Guruvarya's services: astrology consultation, spiritual counselling, Hanuman Upasana guidance, Dasha Mahavidya sadhana guidance.
●	Present his achievements, recognitions, and association with dignitaries/saints credibly.
●	Give Sarvatmak Maharudra Parivar Trust a dedicated space (page/section) that reflects its existing website (sarvatmakmaharudra.org) and social work.
●	Enable easy contact via phone, WhatsApp, email, and a consultation booking form.
●	Ensure the site works well on mobile, since most devotees will access it via phone.
5. Target Audience
Audience	Needs from the Website
Devotees / General Public	Learn about Guruvarya, his spiritual journey, and seek blessings/guidance
Astrology & Counselling Clients	Understand services offered and book a consultation easily
Donors / Trust Supporters	Learn about Sarvatmak Maharudra Parivar Trust's work and how to contribute
Dignitaries / Media	View achievements, recognitions, and credibility markers
Existing Followers	Access pravachan/articles, event updates, and daily wisdom

 9. Brand & Design Requirements
9.1 Logo
Guruvarya's circular portrait photograph is to be used as the website logo — displayed small in the navigation bar and, at a larger size, as the featured hero-section portrait.
 
9.2 Colour Palette
Colour	Hex	Usage
Deep Maroon	#5C1119	Primary background accents, headers, footer
Saffron / Orange	#E8622C	Primary CTA colour, highlights (matches Guruvarya's shawl)
Gold	#C9A227	Borders, icons, premium accents, awards
Cream	#FBF3E7	Page background (warm off-white, not pure white)
Charcoal	#241C1A	Body text
9.3 Typography
●	Headings (Devanagari + display): Tiro Devanagari Marathi / Noto Serif Devanagari — elegant, supports Marathi script
●	Accent / italic taglines: Cormorant Garamond
●	Body text: Poppins — clean, readable, bilingual-friendly
9.4 Visual Tone
●	Warm, divine, premium, calm, and trustworthy — temple-meets-modern-website feel
●	Subtle motifs: Om (ॐ) symbol, mandala patterns, lotus petals, temple-arch shapes
●	Generous white/cream space; gold-line dividers instead of heavy borders
●	Photography should stay warm and devotional in style
 10. Functional Requirements
Feature	Description
Consultation Booking Form	Modal/pop-up form — Name, Phone, Email, Query type, Message — accessible from every page
WhatsApp Quick Connect	Floating button on all pages, linking directly to WhatsApp chat
Click-to-Call	Phone numbers clickable on mobile devices
Gallery Filter	Category-based filtering of gallery photographs
FAQ Accordion	Expand/collapse question list on Home page
Animated Impact Counters	Numbers count up when the Trust section scrolls into view
Responsive Navigation	Collapsible menu for mobile/tablet screens
External Link to Trust Website	'Visit Trust Website' button opens sarvatmakmaharudra.org in a new tab
11. Non-Functional Requirements
●	Responsive design — must work cleanly on mobile, tablet, and desktop (mobile-first, since most devotees will visit via phone)
●	Fast loading — optimised images, minimal blocking scripts
●	Bilingual content support — Marathi/Hindi (Devanagari) and English
●	SEO-friendly — proper page titles, meta descriptions, and use of relevant keywords (e.g. astrologer in Pune, spiritual guru Maharashtra, Hanuman Upasana)
●	Accessibility — legible font sizes/contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support
●	Secure form handling for contact and booking submissions
●	Cross-browser support — latest versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox
